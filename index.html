How to verify

Scan the code. Check the source.

No training needed. Open Glouf, point your phone at the code on the pack, and see whether it matches the registered producer.

  1. Open GloufUse the Glouf app on any smartphone.
  2. Scan the code on the packPoint the camera at the Glouf QR code.
  3. See who registered itGlouf checks the code against the producer's record.
  4. Buy, or walk awayIf there's no match, don't buy it, and report the seller.
