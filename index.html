02How to verify

Scan the code. Check the source.

Your customer doesn't need training. They open Glouf, point their phone at the code on your pack, and see whether it matches you as the registered producer.

  1. Open GloufUse the Glouf app, or visit glouf.com on any phone.
  2. Scan the code on the packPoint the camera at the Glouf QR code.
  3. See who registered itGlouf checks the code against the producer's record.
  4. Buy, or walk awayIf there's no match, don't buy it, and report the seller.
03For manufacturers

Get your products on Glouf in four steps.

Register once, generate codes for the products you want to protect, and apply them to your packaging. No new machinery needed: if you can print a label, you can use Glouf.

Step 1

Create an account

Download the Glouf app or sign up at glouf.com.

Step 2

Sign up as a merchant

Register your company as a producer and add the products you want to protect.

Step 3

Get your QR codes

Glouf generates unique codes linked to each registered product.

Step 4

Paste it on your product

Print the codes on labels or packaging and apply them before goods leave your floor.
